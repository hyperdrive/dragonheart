## The pack brain (2026-11-15)

User: "The wolves should have a squad brain, just as the soldiers when they
defend against the invading horde."

The pack thinks, not the wolf — `WorldManager._tick_packs`, every 0.4 s per
pack, modelled on the garrison's `_tick_squad`:

| State | Rule |
|---|---|
| muster | a scattered pack gathers on its middle; once 3 (or all, if fewer) stand within 90 px it is RELEASED |
| hunt | the whole pack goes for ONE prey — the deer or stocked pen nearest the pack's middle — each wolf closing from its own side of a ring; aim follows the live target |
| meal | 2 kills (`PACK_MEAL`) feed the pack: back to the den, rest 25 s, muster again |
| at bay | a soldier within 70 px of any packmate becomes the whole pack's target at once (focus, like the garrison) until he falls — then the hunt resumes |

`Wolf.gd` only follows its pack's order (`pack_order`): `pack_hunt` (released)
› `muster` › idle, with the garrison's discipline (`_keep_pack_order`: drop
everything when released, re-pick when the order changes). Each wolf still
bolts from the dragon on its own. This replaces the first version's
per-wolf hunting and 18 s individual rest.

## Spread and the encircling attack; a wolf, not a pony (2026-11-15)

User: "The pack is too dense right now, and there should be some interesting
attack dynamic where they spread out when they attack. Also the wolves look
like pony-dogs. Add a mane?"

- Muster loosened: each wolf keeps to its own side of the pack (an offset by
  `pack_slot`), gathered radius 90 → 140 px.
- The attack: released on prey, the pack ENCIRCLES — each wolf runs to its
  slot on a 125 px ring (×0.7 in depth) round the prey, the ring turned to
  face the pack's approach (`WorldManager.pack_slot_spot`). When 3 stand in
  place (within 34 px) or 4.5 s pass, it STRIKES: the rush ripples round the
  ring, slot by slot, 0.35 s apart, each wolf closing from its own side.
  Soldiers get a straight charge. Film probe: fan-out → strike from three
  sides → two pigs → back to the den.
- Look: shorter legs and a lower body, a deep chest tucking to a lean waist,
  haunches, the head low and forward on a near-level neck, a long wedge
  muzzle, a bushy tail with a pale tip — and a shaggy jagged mane over the
  neck and shoulders with lighter tips.
